How every job runs

From first call to finished roof

Every job follows the same straight line — and it starts with a free inspection, so the repair-or-replace call is based on your roof, not a sales pitch.

Repair is usually the start when…

The problem is isolated — a leak in one spot, missing shingles after wind, or flashing that has failed around an edge or vent. We fix it fast and honestly, so a small problem never becomes a big one.

Replacement comes into the conversation when…

Damage or wear shows up across the roof rather than in one place — after major hail, for example. Then a complete tear-off and re-roof, installed to stand up to Panhandle wind and hail, is the honest recommendation.
